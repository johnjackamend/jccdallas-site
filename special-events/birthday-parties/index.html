FAQs

Have questions?

Can I tour the party spaces before booking?
Absolutely. We would be happy to schedule a tour and review all available birthday party options with you.
Is a deposit required?
Yes. A non-refundable deposit reserves your party date and time. Your reservation is not confirmed until the contract is signed and the deposit is received.
How long is a birthday party?
30 minutes for setup, 2 hours of party time, and 30 minutes for cleanup. A dedicated Party Host is assigned to assist you throughout your event.
What is included with my party?
Reserved party space, tables and chairs, a Party Host, access to the selected party activity, and setup and cleanup assistance. Specific amenities vary by party type.
Does the guest count include the birthday child and family members?
Yes. Everyone attending counts toward the total, including the birthday child, family, JCC members, and non-members.
Do JCC members count toward the guest total?
Yes. A reserved party area is held specifically for your event and staffing is based on total attendance, so members count even though they already have facility access.
What if we exceed the included guest count?
Additional staffing fees may apply depending on party type. For pool parties, additional lifeguards are required once attendance exceeds 25 people.
Can I bring food?
Yes. Outside food is permitted; vegetarian and dairy foods are allowed. Meat products are not permitted.
Can I bring a birthday cake?
Absolutely. We do not provide refrigeration, so bring cakes in a cooler if temperature control is needed.
Can I bring drinks?
Yes. Glass containers and alcoholic beverages are prohibited. Coolers are welcome.
Is ice available?
Yes, on request.
Is there a refrigerator or freezer available?
No. The JCC does not provide refrigerator or freezer space for party food, beverages, cakes, or desserts. Please bring coolers.
Can I decorate the party area?
Yes. Balloons, tablecloths, centerpieces, and banners are popular. Not allowed: nails, screws, staples, confetti, and glitter. All decorations must be removed at the end of the party.
Do I need tablecloths?
Not necessarily. Our children's tables are in excellent condition and generally do not require them.
Do guests need waivers?
Yes. All non-members attending must complete the required waiver before entering the facility. Send us your guest list using the template provided and we will check waiver status and tell you who still needs to complete one.
What happens if it rains during an Outdoor Pool Party?
Outdoor pool parties are subject to weather. There is no indoor backup location because of scheduled programming. If weather closes the outdoor pool, we will work with you to reschedule based on availability.
How does the JCC determine weather closures?
At the first sign of thunder or lightning, or if lightning is tracked within 10 miles, outdoor pools are cleared and stay closed for 30 minutes after the last strike or clap. Heavy rain, wind, or thick fog may also close the pools temporarily if the bottom cannot be clearly seen.
What happens if the pool closes due to a fecal or vomit incident?
We will accommodate your party to the best of our ability. If we cannot, a full refund is issued; if some included features are unavailable, a partial discount applies. If the closure results from your party not following facility policies (double diapering, food in the pool area), no refund or discount is issued and the party must be paid in full.
