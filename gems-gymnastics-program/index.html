What Is GEMS?

The JCC Gems Gymnastics team is a recreationally competitive program focused on instilling confidence, strength, teamwork, and hard work. Team gymnasts practice two to three days a week and compete in the USAG and TAAF leagues, primarily at the Xcel levels. Gymnasts advance at their own pace through individualized coaching in a healthy, nurturing environment, building friendships and skills that last a lifetime.

Team Director: Debbie Weinstein

We are thrilled to welcome back Debbie Weinstein, founder of the GEMS program. Debbie believes gymnastics is a powerful vehicle for developing strong, confident young women, with an emphasis on confidence-building, positive thinking, and overall well-being. Under her guidance each gymnast progresses at her own pace, embracing daily challenges in an environment that fosters lifelong relationships.
